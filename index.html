About Noor Quest

Noor Quest is a gentle Islamic learning adventure for young children. Huda and Sami guide you across Noor Island, a map of seven lands, and every land is a short mini-game.

Trace Arabic letters in the Letter Garden, put the steps of wudu and salah in order, match the five pillars of Islam and pick kind answers in the village. On the Story Shore, hear about Prophet Nuh, Prophet Yunus and Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon them). On Quran Hill, build four short surahs word by word, with transliteration and simple meanings.

Earn up to three stars in every round and collect stickers to decorate your own room. There is no timer and no losing, so children can learn at their own pace.

3 quick tips

  1. Tap the speaker button to have the instructions and stories read aloud.
  2. If you are stuck, try twice and the right answer starts to glow.
  3. Visit My Room to place the stickers you have earned.

For parents

Noor Quest teaches young children the basics of Islam through short, gentle games: Arabic letters, four short surahs, wudu, salah, the five pillars, good manners and stories of the Prophets (peace be upon them).

Quran text is the standard Uthmani script (Hafs), checked word by word against quran.com. Transliteration is a simple reading aid; meanings are simplified for children from the Saheeh International translation. No recitation audio is included, so please recite with your child.

Respect: no Prophet, companion or Allah is ever drawn. Stories are told with scenery, objects and animals, based on the Quran. Sounds are soft chimes only, with no music.

Schools of fiqh: where the Hanafi and Shafi'i ways differ (for example where the hands are placed, how the head is wiped, or what is recited behind an imam), the wording is kept simple and general. Please show your child the way your family and teachers pray. Salah is shown as one rak'ah plus the ending, with the number of rak'ahs for each prayer.

How to play

Map: tap a land on Noor Island, then pick a round. The glowing land is a good next stop.

Letter Garden: trace each letter with your finger, then pop the bubble with the letter you hear named.

Wudu Fountain and Prayer Hall: drag the cards (or tap them) in the right order.

Five Palms Oasis: tap a picture, then tap its name to match the pillars of Islam.

Kind Village: choose the kind and polite answer.

Story Shore: read or listen to a story, then answer three little questions.

Quran Hill: tap the words of each verse in order. Arabic is read from right to left.

Stars and stickers: finish a round for a sticker. Get three stars in every round of a land for a bonus sticker.

Safe: free, with no purchases and no adverts during play. Progress stays on this device.
